Where It Began
The Origin

Born from Resilience.
Built for Legacy.

Merrill Johnson entered real estate on October 19, 2001 — just weeks after September 11th shook the world. While others hesitated, Merrill stepped forward. That decision defined everything that followed.

In his first year, he earned Rookie of the Year at Coldwell Banker. Within a decade, he became the #1 selling agent out of 170 at Keller Williams Atlanta Classic — holding that title from 2011 to 2018. A former CFO, Merrill brought financial precision and business acumen to every transaction, setting him apart from every agent in the room.

Today, as Controlling Partner of KW West Atlanta — the fastest-growing market center in the Keller Williams network — Merrill Johnson isn't just in the game. He is the game.
