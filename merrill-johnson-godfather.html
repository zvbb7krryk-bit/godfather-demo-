The Man Behind the Name

Atlanta's Most Trusted Name in Real Estate

When Merrill Johnson stepped into real estate in October 2001 — weeks after September 11th — most people were stepping back. Not Merrill. That first year he earned Rookie of the Year at Coldwell Banker. That's who he is.

A former CFO turned Top Producer, Merrill brings financial precision, market mastery, and genuine care to every client relationship. He ranked #1 out of 170 agents at KW Atlanta Classic from 2011 to 2018 — not once, seven years straight.

Today as Controlling Partner of KW West Atlanta — the fastest-growing market center in the KW network — Merrill Johnson isn't just shaping careers. He's shaping Atlanta's real estate future, one agent and one family at a time.
